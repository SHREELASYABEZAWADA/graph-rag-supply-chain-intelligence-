Incident Report - September 2025

On September 14, 2025, Acme Metals reported a two-week port delay at their
primary export terminal due to congestion, affecting outbound shipments of
Steel Casing (SKU-A100) bound for Warehouse Dallas. Severity: Medium. This
delay is expected to push replenishment of Warehouse Dallas stock by
approximately 10-14 days, which may impact fulfillment for Northwind Traders
and Contoso Retail, both of whom are served out of Warehouse Dallas.

Incident Report - October 2025

Delta Electronics experienced a component shortage for Circuit Board
(SKU-B204) production in early October 2025 due to a semiconductor supplier
issue upstream. Severity: High. Acme Metals is listed as a secondary
supplier for the same Circuit Board product and was asked to increase
allocation temporarily, but at a higher lead time of 30 days.

Incident Report - November 2025

Warehouse Rotterdam flagged a customs clearance issue in late November 2025
affecting Cotton Fabric (SKU-C305) shipments to Fabrikam Inc. Severity: Low.
Resolved within five business days with no material fulfillment impact.
